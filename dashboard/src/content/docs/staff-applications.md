# Staff applications

**Category:** 👥 Community Settings · **Recommended:** yes

Let members apply for staff positions with your own questions. Each application lands in a staff channel, where your reviewers claim it, read the answers and accept or deny it. Accepting gives the position's roles automatically.

## Quick setup

1. Switch on **Staff applications** on the dashboard.
2. In **Settings**, pick a staff-only **review channel** and your **reviewer roles**.
3. In **Positions & panel**, pick the channel for the apply panel and set up your positions: name, questions and the roles to give when accepted.
4. Click **Save and post**. The panel appears with a button per position.

## How applying works

1. A member presses a position's button (or picks it from the dropdown).
2. They answer its questions in a pop-up form. Positions with more than 5 questions use several forms, with a **Continue** button between them (up to 15 questions).
3. The application is posted in your review channel, with the answers, the member's account age and when they joined.

Members can only have one application per position waiting at a time.

## Reviewing

Each application in the review channel has three buttons:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| 🙋 Claim | Shows the other reviewers you're handling it. |
| ✅ Accept | Gives the position's roles and messages the applicant. You can add a note. |
| ❌ Deny | Messages the applicant. You can add a note, like what to work on. |

You can also accept and deny from the **Applications** tab on the dashboard, with the same result.

With **discussion threads** on, each application gets a thread so reviewers can talk it over. The thread closes when it's decided.

## Positions

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Taking applications | Off greys out the button, so members see the position but can't apply yet. |
| Questions | Up to 15. Short (one line) or long (paragraph), each required or optional. |
| Roles to give | Given when an application is accepted (up to 5). |
| Required roles | Only members with one of these roles can apply. |

## Who can apply

In **Settings** you can require a minimum Discord account age and time in the server, and make denied members wait a number of days before applying for the same position again.

## Messages to the applicant

The accept and deny DMs can use these placeholders: `{user}`, `{username}`, `{server}` and `{position}`. A reviewer's note is added at the end. If the applicant's DMs are closed, the reviewer is told so they can reach out another way.

## Permissions

- In the panel and review channels I need **View Channel**, **Send Messages** and **Embed Links**.
- For discussion threads, **Create Public Threads** in the review channel.
- To give roles, **Manage Roles**, and my role must be above the roles I give.
