# Announcements

**Category:** 🛠️ Utilities & Automation · **Recommended:** yes

Write announcements on the dashboard, ping who needs to know, and send them now or schedule them for later.

## Quick setup

1. Switch on **Announcements** on the dashboard.
2. Click **New announcement**, pick a channel and write it.
3. Choose who to ping.
4. Click **Send now**, or pick a time and click **Schedule**.

## Writing

Start from:

- **Blank**
- One of **your server's templates**
- A **starter**: Server update, Event, Maintenance or Rules update

Each announcement has text plus an optional embed (on by default) with its own builder and live preview: colour, author, title, description, up to 25 fields, images, footer and time.

## Pings

| Ping | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| None | Default. |
| @everyone | Pings every member. |
| @here | Pings members who are online. |
| Roles | Up to 10 roles. |

Only the chosen ping goes off, even if other mentions are in the text. Admins can only use pings they're allowed to use themselves.

## Publishing

In **announcement channels**, tick **Publish** to send the announcement on to every server that follows the channel.

## Scheduling

- Send at a set time, up to **90 days** ahead.
- Up to **25** scheduled announcements waiting per server.
- Number One checks every 30 seconds, so scheduled announcements still go out after a restart.
- Scheduled announcements can be **edited**, **sent right away** or **cancelled**.

## Templates

- Save announcements you send often as templates, with their channel and ping. Up to 25 per server.
- Click **Save as template** while writing to save the current one.
- A template's channel is picked automatically when you use it. Leave it empty to choose each time.

## History

- The last 30 sent announcements are listed with links to them.
- Sent announcements can be **edited** (the ping isn't sent again) or **deleted**.
- Failed ones show why they failed in plain words and can be **tried again**.

## Permissions

- **View Channel**, **Send Messages** and **Embed Links** in the channel.
- **Mention @everyone, @here and All Roles** for those pings.
