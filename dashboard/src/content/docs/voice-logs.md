# Voice logs

**Category:** 📋 Logging & Auditing · **Recommended:** yes

Posts voice channel activity to a staff channel: who joined, left or moved, and server mutes and deafens by staff.

## Quick setup

1. Create a log channel that only staff can see.
2. Switch on **Voice logs** and pick that channel.
3. Click **Save changes**.

Nothing is logged until a log channel is picked.

## What's logged

| Event | Default | What the log shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Joins | On | Who joined which voice channel. |
| Leaves | On | Who left, and **how long they stayed**. |
| Moves | On | Moves from one voice channel to another. |
| Server mutes and deafens | On | Mutes and deafens done by staff. People muting or deafening themselves aren't logged. |

## Skipping things

| Setting | Default | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skip bots | On | Music bots and other bots aren't logged. |
| Skipped channels | None | Up to 50 voice channels or whole categories. |

## Permissions

**View Channel**, **Send Messages** and **Embed Links** in the log channel.
